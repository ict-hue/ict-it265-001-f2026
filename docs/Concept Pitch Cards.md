# IT265 Module 2: Concept Pitch Cards

Copy this card for each of your two or three shortlisted concepts. Aim to explain each in about one minute. Use your [idea bank](./01-idea-bank.md) as a starting point.

## Pitch Card 1

**Working title:** Bioslam

**Player role and situation:** A rouge biomass has taken control of some of a scientist's possessions! The scientist must make a new biomass, utilizing the creatures he has left to destroy is rouge creation before it's too late!

**Repeated decision or action:** Card playing, akin to mtg

**Goal, pressure, or ending:** Destroy the other player by summoning creatures and using them for pressure.

**Hook:** Creatures in your graveyard are able to be stitched together at the cost of max energy.

**Smallest useful physical prototype:** deck of cards, d20s
