Card game:

Ready to pitch

* Creature commanding card game  
* Focuses on exploiting different stats of characters  
* Heavy emphasis on card sacrifice  
  * 3 Dead cards can be stitched together using attack, health, and sigils  
    * Stitched creatures are used as an effective shield  
    * The cost is determined by the cost of the creatures, and harms one’s own energy generation  
* Energy is incremental (+1 max energy spent per turn), reanimating takes from max energy but does not consume energy itself

Scrabble-like

* Inspired by the game pronoun palace, a scrabble roguelike inspired by bookworm adventures  
* Create words to make attacks; defence tiles are worth double
