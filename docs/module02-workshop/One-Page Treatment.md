# IT265 Module 2: One-Page Treatment

**Working game title:** Stitcher

Write one coherent, one-page-length treatment. Use the four prompts to begin, then combine your answers into natural prose.

## Four-Sentence Core

1. **Premise:** Who is the player, what is the situation, and what problem matters?
2. **Experience and loop:** What should play feel like, and what does the player repeatedly decide or do?
3. **Goal and pressure:** What changes, what limits the player, and how does play end?
4. **Scope:** What makes this version distinctive, and what can you test first with affordable physical materials?

## Treatment Draft

One player is Dr. Frankfurter, and the other is his failed biomass experiment; the experiment has taken over half of his lab, and he needs to "assemble" his own army to fight back. Both players draft a deck of 20 cards, and try to amass a battlefield to attack the other biomass while defending themselves. Both players can also stitch together creatures from their graveyard, allowing them to create an amalgamation of power, health, and sigils to serve as a "body" for their biomass; the goal is to destroy the other player. Energy management is key; energy regenerates every turn and increments, but max energy is sacrificed for stitching. With the rulesets in mind, the game can be made functional as long as the cards are made.
